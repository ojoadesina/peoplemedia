# Homepage

- Two tabs: **People** (Alex, Daniel…) and **Relationships** (Mum, Sister…).
- Both are simple lists of people. Names only by default.
- **Never a feed.** People-first, not content-first.
- A person with an active round shows their round name, frame, mood, and activities boxes.
- Sorted by recency of round — creating a round pulls you to the front.
- After expiry the person stays in place. Not demoted. Stays until someone else overtakes.
- **Pinned:** up to 10, always visible, never collapsed.
- Tabs set the audience for a new round.

# Round

Deliberately created. A captured ongoing or recent moment — not a stream.

- Round is a way of expressing yourself and seeking interaction. It's what fulfils *so you don't do life alone*, more than presence can.
- Works with or without name, frame, mood, activities. All optional.
- Name behaves like a forum title, character-limited.
- **Public** — to everyone. **Private** — to a specific person or scoped people, who are notified on creation.
- Expires 45 minutes after the **creator** goes quiet. Not other participants.
- Expiry removes the boxes and the name from the row. It doesn't close the room — words keep flowing, notifications keep arriving.
- **A round surfaces a person. It doesn't contain the conversation.**
- Doesn't re-surface after expiry. Going round again is one tap.
- Attributes attached to a person create a round. Attached to a word, they're enclosures.

# Going round

- **Plus button.** Not inline click — misclick risk, and double-click is friction.
- Replaces the top marked area with the creation UI in place. No panel.
- Manual cancel, or send as is.
- Audience comes from the tab you're on.

# Frame

- A captured moment. Captures **voice, face, or state**. **Capture only, never upload.**
- Attaches to a round, or to a word.
- On a round, the frame is **pinned to that round** — what it's about. Never updated from inside.
- A round frame, unfilled or capturing, shows a **pulsating effect**.
- Permanent. Frames accumulate; nothing is overwritten.
- Owned by whoever captured it, so all of a person's frames can be pulled later.
- Wears its age.

# Mood

- Colour plus plain text — satisfactory, joyful, sad, lonely. No emoji, no icons.
- Exists independently; can be set on its own.
- On rounds only. Not on words.
- Optional.

# Activities

- Simple category: food, travelling, rest, movie.
- Exists independently; can be set on its own.
- Optional.

# Word

- Speech plus attached documents. **The only place uploads are allowed.**
- Can carry a frame.
- Threaded — it's chat, and that's fine.
- Notifies in private rounds. **Public rounds never notify.**

# A person's page

- Clicking someone takes you to their **latest round** — active if they have one, otherwise their most recent.
- Past rounds sit collapsed below. Tap to expand and pick from the list.
- Has a **non-round, non-word frame** that acts as a bio.
- Shows all rounds between you and them, yours and theirs, interleaved by time. One conversation, one place.

# Look

- If you stay on someone's page a while, the creator is notified: *X is around you, will you go round publicly or with them?*
- Named inside a mutual scope. Aggregate only outside it.

# Presence

- Live. The primary way of seeing people.
- Frame does not attach to it.

# Laws

1. Absence is silent. Nothing notifies about absence — no expiry notice, no "nobody joined", no "X hasn't replied."
2. No counters on anything relational.
3. Everything pushed must be something a human did toward a person.
4. Expiry is about visibility, never deletion.
5. Frames are captured. Words are made.

# Notes — built differently from the guide above

- **The doing is on the row, not in a box.** The guide puts name, frame, mood
  and activity all in the boxes beside the band. Three of them stayed there; the
  doing came out, because boxes answer one person at a time and you have to
  settle somebody to read them. A list you must interrogate person by person is
  a list nobody scrolls twice. So the doing sits under the name, grey and small
  — the name is what you scan for, the doing is what makes you stop — and the
  boxes are two: **mood** and **frame**.

# Notes — decided, not yet built

- **Plus while you are on someone's page.** Do not close their page and do not
  send them back. It means a round *with that person* — strictly private. Close
  their current round, expand the collapsed past rounds, and insert the creation
  tool at the top of that list. The rounds already share the same UI, so it fits.
  Held until the person's page is properly designed.
